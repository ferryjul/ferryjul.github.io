01 / ABOUT

Short bio.

I am a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Mathematics and Industrial Engineering at Polytechnique Montréal, in the SCALE-AI Chair in Data-Driven Supply Chains. I work with Prof. Thibaut Vidal on trustworthy machine learning and optimization.

I earned my PhD in 2023 at LAAS-CNRS / Université Toulouse III Paul Sabatier, where I studied the interplays between interpretability, fairness, and privacy in machine learning using combinatorial optimization and operations research.

My research focuses on trustworthy machine learning, with a focus on certifiability, interpretability, explainability, fairness, robustness, privacy, and the relationships among these goals. My works draw primarily on combinatorial optimization, particularly mathematical programming, to develop methods with rigorous guarantees of optimality or constraint satisfaction.

02 / RESEARCH

Questions that drive my work.

Combinatorial optimization provides powerful and flexible tools for jointly modelling and studying many desiderata related to trustworthy ML. This allows thorough characterizations, with rigorous guarantees on optimality and constraint satisfaction.

01

Privacy & model security

What can a trained model—or an explanation of one of its decisions—disclose about its training data? I develop reconstruction attacks and study defenses for structured models.

02

Counterfactual explanations

How can we find meaningful changes that alter a model’s prediction? I study exact and efficient methods for counterfactual explanations in tree ensembles.

03

Fair & interpretable learning

How do accuracy, fairness, transparency, and sparsity interact? I design fair and interpretable learning methods and provably characterize the space of good models for several hypothesis classes.

04

Hybrid interpretable models

How to jointly leverage the predictive power of a black-box and the transparent nature of a simple model? My research studies optimization-grounded methods to learn such models and characterize the novel chanllenges they carry.

05

Faithful compression

How to compress an existing model without altering its prediction function? My work uses tailored row- and column-generation frameworks to compress existing tree ensembles while certifiably preserving their decision boundaries.

06

Robustness

How to make sure that a trained model will perform well outside its training data distribution? I use distributionally robust optimization tools grounded in combinatorial optimization to derive rigorous worst-case guarantees.

Improving one trustworthy ML desiderata is likely to affect others: only a joint handling of all desirable properties can make ML really trustworthy.
